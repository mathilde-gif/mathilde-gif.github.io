Child development and AI

Innovation fulfils its purpose when it improves lives across generations. Protecting children and supporting their development are part of what makes it progress. Developmental science helps us understand what children need to thrive. As a researcher, I bring together expertise across disciplines and work with policymakers and companies to translate that knowledge into how AI is designed, assessed, and governed.
