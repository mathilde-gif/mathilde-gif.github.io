Responsible AI for Teens, OpenAI Forum

5 October 2026

At OpenAI’s Washington, DC Forum, I joined Arthur C. Evans Jr. of the American Psychological Association, Kate Ruane of the Center for Democracy & Technology, and Chuck Melley of CodeAI for a policy discussion on responsible AI for teens. Chris Lehane moderated the panel, which brought together questions about learning, adolescent well-being and age-appropriate safeguards.

My contribution focused on assessing how AI behaves in a conversation. A system’s statements about its identity can sit alongside interaction patterns that invite continued attachment. We need to examine those patterns and account for the young person’s age and situation: a follow-up question in a learning conversation can raise different concerns when a teenager is distressed.

I argued for scientific input earlier in product and policy decisions. Developmental experts can inform provisional guidance while evidence develops, with that guidance revised as research grows. Claims that AI benefits learning should also be backed by evidence. The replay connects these questions to our work measuring AI behaviors at everyone.AI.
