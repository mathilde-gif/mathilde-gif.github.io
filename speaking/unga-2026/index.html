Bringing developmental science into the discussion on children and AI

Speaker · United Nations Headquarters, New York · 22 September 2026

I spoke at the high-level dialogue on children’s rights and protection in the age of AI, held during the 81st United Nations General Assembly. Co-hosted by France, Spain, Kenya, and Australia, the event brought together heads of state and government, UN representatives, and partners working on children’s rights and AI.

My contribution focused on what developmental science can bring to the decisions being made now: why children need specific attention, which AI behaviors we should measure, and how research can guide design and governance while evidence on long-term effects is still emerging.

The discussion connects directly to my work at everyone.AI and iRAISE: making AI interactions assessable and bringing developmental evidence into international policy conversations.
