Governments & international organisations

I translate developmental science into AI policy priorities, drawing on engagements with the French government and discussions at UNGA, the Paris Peace Forum and AI for Good. Topics include age-appropriate AI, emotional and relational risks, and international cooperation while evidence develops.

Formats: Keynotes, high-level panels, policy briefings, roundtables and moderation.

Watch UNGA on UN Web TV ↗

Technology companies & builders

I speak to frontier AI labs, startups and product teams about child development and AI design. Topics include learning tools, conversational companions, anthropomorphism, emotional reliance, autonomy and critical thinking, and how to assess AI behaviors to inform product decisions.

Formats: Keynotes, industry panels, executive briefings and product workshops.

Watch OpenAI Forum · Responsible AI for Teens ↗

Universities & professional learning

I connect learning, cognition and social relationships with responsible and ethical AI. My contributions include guest lecturing in “AI and the Future of Business” at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business and the Education Futures Fellowship, hosted by Svenia Busson.

Formats: Guest lectures, research seminars, masterclasses and interactive workshops.

Explore the research behind CALIBER →

Schools, educators & families

I work with school districts, educators, students and parents on growing up with AI. Topics include independent thinking, motivation, creativity, emotional attachment and AI literacy. Sessions address practical choices at school and at home, adapted to young people’s ages and the adults supporting them.

Formats: Professional development, parent talks, student sessions, keynotes and interactive Q&As.

Biography and headshot for organizers

A short introduction

Dr. Mathilde Cerioli is a cognitive neuroscientist, Co-founder and Chief Scientist of everyone.AI, and Chief Scientist of iRAISE. Her work connects child development, AI evaluation, and public policy. She leads the development of CALIBER, a method for assessing AI behaviors, and contributes to international discussions on AI and children. Her speaking engagements include a high-level dialogue at United Nations Headquarters during UNGA 2026, the Paris Peace Forum, and the AI Action Summit.
