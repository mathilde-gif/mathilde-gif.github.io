Lighting the Future: Ethical AI for the Next Generation

11 April 2026

At San Francisco’s Night of Ideas, I joined “Lighting the Future: Ethical AI for the Next Generation” with Jeff Hancock, Allison Fine Mishkin and Gregory Renard. Anne-Sophie Seret moderated the conversation, with youth advocate Nikhil Gujral introducing the panel. The evening was open to the public and brought research, product policy and young people’s perspectives together.

We discussed how AI could support learning and development, and what responsible design requires: safer defaults, clear guardrails and independent evaluation. The conversation considered how these systems may shape attention, behavior and trust, alongside the opportunities they offer young people.

This is part of how I make developmental research accessible to audiences beyond specialist conferences. Parents, educators, builders and young people need ways to understand the decisions behind AI systems and take part in the conversation about the technologies entering their lives.
