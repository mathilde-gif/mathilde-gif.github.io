G7, AI and Children: Building Beneficial GenAI for Children

15 June 2026

In Paris, I presented iRAISE’s Mapping of GenAI Impacts on Child Development, which I led with Daniel Hipp. The event, organized by the Paris Peace Forum, everyone.AI and the Human Technology Foundation under the label of France’s G7 Presidency, brought scientific evidence and young people’s perspectives into the same conversation.

The mapping examines anticipated benefits and risks across developmental stages. In my presentation, I highlighted the need to consider autonomy, emotional reliance, cognitive independence and socio-emotional development alongside content safety. Its purpose is to help decision-makers ask more precise questions about the AI children encounter.

Young contributors also presented the iRAISE Youth Manifesto, bringing forward the perspectives of 108 young people from 22 countries. Government, industry and child-rights participants discussed age-appropriate design and safeguards. The report and replay document how research and youth perspectives can inform these shared decisions.
