AI’s Impact on Children: Scrolling to death Podcast

12 December 2024

I joined Nicki Petrossi on Scrolling 2 Death to discuss how AI is entering children's lives and why developmental knowledge should inform the systems they encounter. The episode brings that question back to the decisions families face today.

We discuss the appeal of AI interactions, the importance of face-to-face relationships, and the need for AI developers and child development experts to work together. The conversation also offers practical starting points for parents who want to talk with their children about AI and its risks.

Resources accompanying the episode include our report, The Future of Child Development in the AI Era, and Common Sense Media's AI app reviews. The full 52-minute conversation is available to watch or listen to.
