Biography and headshot for journalists

A short introduction

Dr. Mathilde Cerioli is a cognitive neuroscientist, Co-founder and Chief Scientist of everyone.AI, and Chief Scientist of iRAISE. Her work connects child development, AI evaluation, and public policy. She leads the development of CALIBER, a method for assessing AI behaviors, and contributes to international discussions on AI and children. Her speaking engagements include a high-level dialogue at United Nations Headquarters during UNGA 2026, the Paris Peace Forum, and the AI Action Summit.
